| **Link to future workplace:**<br>Identify values/duties/responsibilities involved<br>1- Explanation: use definitions by negation, by logical reasoning or textbook definitions<br>2- Visualisation: use real-world or hypothetical examples | **Values / duties:** public safety (a), competence (b, f, 3.8), integrity and honesty (c, d), compliance with codes and standards (3.4), accountability for what I sign (3.5), duty to warn (h, 3.6) and duty to report (i, 3.3).<br><br>**1- Explanation.** *Competence* is not a diploma on the wall; by negation, it is the absence of work done beyond your training and experience.
